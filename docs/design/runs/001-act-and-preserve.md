# Run 001 — "The collective acts on M" ∧ "M preserves unresolved tension"

**Status:** complete, residue preserved.
**Chosen because:** this tension was *created* by the answers given in the design
conversation, it is internal to the system rather than about its domain (an `L₃` tension in

## 1 · Expose

**Claim A — act.** The collective acts on `M`. Action selects one course. At the moment of
acting the field collapses to a single realized thing. You cannot half-deploy, half-treat,
half-publish.

> a1 action requires commitment to one course
> a2 commitment requires the field to become single-valued
> a3 unresolved tension blocks action
> a4 deadlines are exogenous and real

**Claim B — preserve.** `M` retains conflicts, uncertainties and unobserved regions.
Collapsing them destroys precisely what makes a collective worth more than one observer.

> b1 preservation and collapse are incompatible operations
> b2 the collective's value lies in what it retains
> b3 premature resolution is the failure this design exists to prevent
> b4 residue is recoverable only if never discarded

Apparent form: at time `t`, `M` must be both collapsed and uncollapsed.

## 3 · Dichotomy audit (D2t)

|  | preserves | ¬preserves |
|---|---|---|
| **acts** | **?** | vote → act → forget (the standard consensus system) |
| **¬acts** | the paralysis failure mode already flagged in `06` | inert |

Search the `acts ∧ preserves` cell for occupants:

- **immune response** — commits to a response while retaining the full receptor repertoire;
  it does not delete what it did not use
- **canary / staged rollout** — acts, and the action is *designed* to preserve the
  alternative
- **hedged portfolio** — acts on several positions at once wherever actions are compatible
- **clinical treatment with a stopping rule** — commits, and preserves the alternative as a
  documented next line
- **any organism at all** — every regulatory act is a commitment that leaves the regime
  repertoire intact (D1 §7)

Cell heavily occupied. **Downgrade: distinct, not opposed.**

**Generative question:** what distinguishes occupants of `acts ∧ preserves` from occupants
of `acts ∧ ¬preserves`? Every occupant shares one property: the action does not foreclose
its alternatives.

> **Recovered dimension: foreclosure, not decisiveness.**

The binary was collapsing a two-dimensional space — *how firmly do you commit* and *how
much do you destroy* — onto one axis.

## 4 · Localize the minimal inconsistent set

```
I = { the collective acts on M,
      a2  commitment requires M to become single-valued,
      b1  preservation and collapse are incompatible }
```

Minimal: drop `a2` and the inconsistency dissolves; drop `b1` and it dissolves. Neither is
an observation. Both are theories about the *relation* between representation and action.

**Structural echo.** In the organism run, the conflict was not between biological
observations but between change and a particular *theory of identity*. Here it is not
between acting and knowing but between them and a particular *theory of decision*.

> **Hypothesis (n = 2, offered as a probe not a law):** contradictions tend to localize in
> the theory of the relation, not in either relatum. If it survives more runs it is a
> useful default for step 4 — look at the connective first.

## 5 · Decompress "act"

```
A = (V, B, L, E, K, W)
```

`V` reversibility · `B` blast radius · `L` latency to feedback · `E` foreclosure ·
`K` collapse required · `W` who bears the consequence

| Action class | `V` | `E` | `K` |
|---|---|---|---|
| probe / canary | high | ≈ 0 | ≈ 0 |
| reversible commitment | high | low | partial |
| costly to reverse | low | medium | high |
| foreclosing | none | total | total |

**`K` is not constant.** "The collective acts" is not one operation with one cost, and
that is the whole resolution.

## 6 · Perturb the assumptions

**Model 1 — full collapse.** `M` collapses at every action. Classical decision theory.
Consequence: no memory of alternatives, every action restarts deliberation, and the system
cannot learn from being wrong because it has forgotten what else it considered. Cheap, and
standard.

**Model 2 — no collapse.** Act on all views at once. Possible only where actions are
compatible — but that is exactly **degeneracy (A5)**: several different implementations
pursuing one function simultaneously. So Model 2 is available more often than it looks, and
A5 is how you buy it.

**Model 3 — scoped collapse.** `M` collapses only along the dimensions the action touches,
only for the duration of the commitment. Viable.

**Model 4 — collapse as projection.** The action is `π_a(M)`; the fiber `π_a⁻¹(a)` is
everything `M` contained that the action did not distinguish. **The residue is the fiber.**
B2's machinery already covers it — no new concept, no new storage.

**Model 5 — action as regime (A4).** The collective enters an action regime and can return.
Collapse is a mode, not a destruction.

Models 3–5 are mutually compatible and jointly viable.

## 7 · New distinctions

Three kinds of resolution, parallel to state / pattern / trajectory identity:

1. **Epistemic resolution** — the question is settled. Rare. The only one that updates
   `epistemic_status`.
2. **Operational collapse** — a course is selected; `M` is unchanged. Common. This is
   D5t's `operational_decision`.
3. **Structural foreclosure** — the possibility is destroyed. The action removes states
   from the space anyone can ever observe.

The third was missing from the design. Using D6 §8 — `dX/dt = G(x, X)`, the possibility
space is itself state — an irreversible action does not merely commit resources. **It
deletes regions of `X`.**

> **`epistemic_cost(a) = measure of hypotheses rendered permanently untestable by a`**

Risk frameworks price expected loss. None price destroyed falsifiability. This gives "keep
your options open" an epistemic justification rather than merely an economic one, and makes
the cost publishable alongside expected value.

## 8 · Unification: action and probe are one category

- a **probe** is an action with `E ≈ 0`
- an **action** is a probe with consequences

One type, carrying `(collapse cost, information yield)`. Prefer, among actions of
comparable expected value, the highest yield per unit foreclosure.

This operationalizes D2 §16 — living systems survive by ignoring most detail while
preserving the distinctions that matter *for action* — because the action-relevant
projection is now indexed to the action's own reversibility.

**Rule:** under uncertainty, prefer the action that is also a probe. Not out of caution:
it is the only class of action that *increases* `M` while acting on it.

## 9 · Who collapses? — the human/AI distinction, applied

Collapse authority is where the non-collapse rule from `07` does the most work.

The relevant asymmetry is **not competence**. It is that foreclosure and
consequence-bearing are coupled, and only one kind of participant currently stands in the
consequence-bearing relation.

| Foreclosure `E` | Consequence-bearer `W` | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| ≈ 0 (probe-class) | any | any participant, or automatic |
| low | internal | AI participant may collapse; logged with reopening trigger |
| high | internal | human authority required |
| any | **external** | human authority required |
| total | any | human authority **plus** published epistemic-cost statement |

Derived by function, not status. The alternative framing — *humans decide because humans
are more reliable* — is false on most projections and would collapse a different
distinction while claiming to preserve this one.

## 10 · Paraconsistent status

`v(P) = (1, 1)` — supported under operational collapse, opposed under epistemic resolution.

## 11 · Boundary

Can the system determine, from within, how much foreclosure requires whose authority?

**No.** The threshold is purpose-relative in exactly the way the organism run's identity
criterion was:

```
C_safety         minimize foreclosure of recoverable states
C_velocity       minimize time to commitment
C_learning       maximize information per unit foreclosure
C_accountability consequence-bearers hold collapse authority
C_autonomy       maximize participants' capacity to act without collective approval
```

There is no assumption-free choice among these.

> **The system can measure what an action forecloses. It cannot derive, from within, how
> much foreclosure requires whose authority.**

Structurally D7t: not derivable in `T`; a meta-level criterion must be introduced, and its
introduction must remain visible. Not Gödel's theorem — the same operational posture.

## 14 · The field

The binary became:

> **reversible in `V` · bounded in `B` · partial in `K` · nearly zero in `E` ·
> internal in `W` · fast in `L`**

— a region in a six-dimensional action space, not a point on an act/preserve axis. Most
actions worth taking live in the corner where `E` is small and `K` is partial, and that
corner was invisible while the question was "act or preserve?"
