# 00 — Premises

The three source documents look like separate topics. They are not. This file records the
claims each contributes and the three unifications that collapse them into one system.

---

## D1 — Identity is a maintained regime

> *identity = the regulated continuity of a self-producing process*

- A participant is not defined by a fixed state, config, or schema version. It is defined
  by a **viability region** Ω — the family of states compatible with still being itself.
  `x(t) ∈ Ω`, never `x(t) = x(0)`.
- Every participant maintains a **boundary** that is selectively permeable. Fully closed
  starves; fully open dissolves. Both are death. Identity lives in *regulated openness*.
- Components are continually regenerated by the organization they in turn constitute.
  Material turnover does not break identity, because the surrounding organization
  re-specifies what replaces it.
- Regulation has **movable setpoints** (allostasis): stability achieved through
  appropriate change, not through fixed targets.
- Identity is **distributed across scales**. No single component carries the whole
  pattern. Coordination among levels *is* the identity.
- Stability is an **attractor repertoire**, not a fixed point. Identity is the set of
  transformations a system can undergo and still return to viable organization.
- **Degeneracy** (different means, same function) creates resilience that duplication
  cannot.
- **Repair restores relationships, not the past.** Scar tissue is a valid outcome.
- **Development preserves identity by transforming it.** The invariant is transformational
  continuity, not shape.

## D2 — The geometry of observation is not the geometry of the system

> *emergence = structured compression that retains consequential relations*

- Everything is seen through a projection `y = π(x)`, which is many-to-one. Behind every
  observation sits a **fiber** `π⁻¹(y)` of states it cannot distinguish.
- A higher level is *autonomous* only when the projection approximately commutes:
  `π ∘ F_t ≈ G_t ∘ π`. When it fails, the macrovariables are incomplete — and the failure
  mode menu is explicit (more variables, history, different timescale, better projection,
  stochastic terms, environmental coupling).
- Lost dimensions manufacture artifacts: apparent trajectory crossings, false discreteness
  from thresholds, distorted distance, reversed causal associations under aggregation.
- Discarded variables **return as memory** (path dependence) or **as noise** (unresolved
  deterministic structure). Neither is a property of the system; both are properties of
  the projection.
- Populations are not average individuals. `{0,0,10,10}` and `{5,5,5,5}` share a mean and
  share nothing else. What survives the scale transition is often **correlation**, not
  magnitude.
- Many observers with genuinely different projections shrink the ambiguity region:
  `x ∈ ⋂ πᵢ⁻¹(yᵢ)`. **Agreement suggests an invariant; disagreement suggests a hidden
  dimension** — parallax.
- Agreement between *correlated* observers reproduces one blind spot n times.
- Synthesis is itself a projection. There is no view from nowhere.

## D3 — Paradox is a typed rupture, not an error

> *preserve tension → expose its structure → test its assumptions → extract new dimensions*

- Contradiction, undecidability, uncomputability, self-reference, ontological mismatch,
  indexical mismatch, equivocation, and false dichotomy all *feel* the same and require
  different responses. Typing comes before response.
- **Opposition must be demonstrated, not assumed.** Two claims differing is not two claims
  conflicting.
- Contradiction must not explode. A paraconsistent layer keeps local inconsistency local.
- Truth, provability, computability, and ontological compatibility are **independent
  axes**. Flattening them into "unknown" destroys the information.
- Gödel: unresolvability is *relative to a stated theory* `T = (A, R, L)`. Moving to `T⁺`
  is legitimate but must remain visible, and `T⁺` acquires its own limits.
- Turing: "no general procedure exists" does not imply "this instance is unreachable."
  Bounded methods with published bounds are the correct response.
- Reflexivity is necessary but must be **level-marked and bounded**, or it is paralysis.
- Tension must **produce something** — a distinction, a variable, a bridge, a probe — or
  it is only bookkeeping.

## The three unifications

These are the load-bearing observations. Without them this is three separate frameworks
sharing a repository.

### 1. The membrane *is* the projection

D1 gives each participant a selective boundary. D2 gives each participant a lossy
projection `πᵢ`. D3's §16 states it outright: receptors *select particular differences and
map them into internal signals*.

They are the same object seen from two sides:

- **Inbound**, the membrane decides which environmental differences become internal signal
  — that is `π` applied to the world.
- **Outbound**, it decides which internal differences become visible to others — that is
  `π` applied to the self.

Consequence: `participants/<name>/membrane/` and `projections/πᵢ` are one artifact, not
two. A participant's admission policy and its epistemic signature are the same
declaration. And the viability report from D1 is itself a projection — a participant
reporting `healthy` has a fiber of undistinguished states behind it, and every blind-spot
rule applies to its own health.

### 2. A false dichotomy *is* an apparent crossing

D2 §4: two trajectories appear to intersect because a dimension was projected away.
D3 §10: two positions appear to be exhaustive opposites because the possibility space was
projected onto one axis.

Identical phenomenon, different space — state space versus claim space. The
dimension-restoration machinery serves both, so the false-dichotomy detector is not a new
subsystem; it is the apparent-crossing detector pointed at claims.

### 3. D1 is the bridge D3 asks for

D3's worked ontology example: participant A says *a cell is an object* (boundary,
persistence, countability); participant B says *a cell is a process* (turnover,
self-production, temporal continuity). D3 asks for a bridge `B` connecting the two
ontologies without forcing a merge.

D1's thesis is that bridge: *a self-maintaining pattern of constraints imposed on flows of
matter and energy* — a temporally persistent organization of processes with a maintained
boundary.

The three documents mutually resolve. That is weak evidence the frame is coherent rather
than three independently appealing metaphors, and it is the reason this design has one
mechanism catalog instead of three.

---

## The operational consequence of all three

Gödel limits, Turing limits, and self-reference get **identical treatment**: bounded
exploration with an explicitly published boundary. Different mathematics; one discipline.

In each case the failure is never *hitting* the limit. The failure is claiming more than
your theory, your budget, or your level actually supports.
